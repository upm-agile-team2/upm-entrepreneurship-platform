## Why

Students need to find investors whose experience and interests match their project, then see whether that investor can offer relevant support.

## What Changes

- Let students open an investor profile and see their available details and public feedback (US025, #96; T56–T57, #109 and #110).
- Add a directory with an interest filter, clear action, and no-match state (US026, #97; T58–T59, #111 and #112).

## Capabilities

### New Capabilities

- `investor-profile-discovery`: students can find and view investor profiles.

### Modified Capabilities

None.

## Impact

Uses the data model and editor from T53–T55. Adds student-facing views and a link from visible feedback to its author's investor profile. Messaging and private feedback access are outside this change.
