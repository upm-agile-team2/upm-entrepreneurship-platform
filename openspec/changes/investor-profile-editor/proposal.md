## Why

Students need information about investors' experience and interests to decide whom to approach. The first part of H4 lets investors create, update, and review that information.

## What Changes

- Add an investor profile and its interest tags (T53, #106).
- Let investors save and update their profile (T54, #107) through an authenticated editor and preview (T55, #108).
- Cover the acceptance criteria of US024 (#95). Student access, feedback history, and discovery are planned separately in US025 and US026.

## Capabilities

### New Capabilities

- `investor-profiles`: an investor can create, edit, and review their own profile.

### Modified Capabilities

None.

## Impact

Adds an accounts migration, editor form, profile views and templates, and Django acceptance tests.
